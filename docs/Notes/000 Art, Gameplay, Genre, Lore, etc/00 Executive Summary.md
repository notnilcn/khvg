# High Concept
A recent world-wide magic-related catastrophe that caused 75% of the planet to be patchwork-merged with other worlds has sent the world into turmoil and players must venture into dangerous alien landscapes in order to rebuild.
# Demographic
**Target Audience:** MMO players who enjoy difficult skill based games.
**Game Style:** Bullet-hell rogue-like perma-death base-building mmo.
**Platform:** PC
**Concurrent Players:** idk
# Overview
Players spawn into a world where people had set up long range world wide teleportation gates, however, these gates malfunctioned catastrophically and caused 75% of the planet to be patchwork-merged with other worlds which contain dangerous monsters.

This rogue-like bullet-hell perma-death base-building mmo is predominately viewed from a top-down angle, however, players will be able to rotate and zoom the camera along any angle. The core gameplay is heavily inspired by Realm of the Mad God, however, there will be elements of the game that are inspired by BitCraft Online, Clash of Clans, and Factorio (currently deferred).

This game will use realistically-proportional low-poly 3d-models that're passed through a pixel art shader to give the game a realistic but stylized look.

==The story of this game is peripheral to the gameplay. I have ideas for a story but that's it.==
The story will follow 3 characters who played a large role in the lead up to the catastrophe. Players will be able to pick a side, but there wont be any faction PvP like in World of Warcraft.

# Selling Points
**Unique gameplay:** There are very little games out there that are similar to Realm of the Mad God. The players of that game have been complaining about the lack of competition that Realm of the Mad God has, and it is a common talking point among them that they would move to a different game if it could replicate RotMG's gameplay. This game will have minor improvements on RotMG's core gameplay, however, the basic idea will be the same.
